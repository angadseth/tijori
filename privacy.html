Tijori privacy

Your documents and passwords, locked.

Short version: Tijori locks everything on your own device with your master password before it goes anywhere. Nobody else, including the maker and Google, can read what you store.

What stays on your device

Your master password and the key made from it never leave the device. Documents are read (OCR) on the device too.

Google sign-in and Drive

If you turn on sync, Tijori asks Google for two things only:

  • Your email address, to show which account the vault syncs with.
  • Its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Tijori cannot see or touch any other file in your Drive.

Only encrypted files go into that folder (XChaCha20-Poly1305, key from Argon2id). They stay in your Google account. Tijori has no server of its own and collects no analytics.

Removing your data

Sign out in Tijori's Settings to stop syncing. To delete the cloud copy, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Tijori → Delete hidden app data.
