Privacy policy

Tijori collects nothing about you.

Your documents and passwords are encrypted on your own device. There is no Tijori server, no account with us and no analytics. This policy explains the few things that do leave your device, and why.

Effective 30 Oct 2026Applies to Tijori for Windows, the phone app and this website

01Who we are

Tijori is made and maintained by Angad Jangir, an individual developer at IIT Madras, India. "We" and "us" in this policy mean him. Tijori is free and earns nothing.

02What stays on your device

Everything you put in Tijori, including documents, photos, passwords, notes and the text read from your documents, is encrypted on your device before it is stored. Your master password and the key made from it never leave the device and are never stored. Documents are read (OCR) on the device too.

We cannot see any of it. There is no server for it to reach.

03Google sign-in and Drive

Sync is optional and off until you turn it on. If you sign in with Google to sync your devices, Tijori asks Google for these permissions only:

PermissionWhat Tijori does with it
openid and emailReads your email address to show which Google account the vault syncs with. It is kept on your device and is not sent anywhere else.
drive.appdataStores your encrypted vault files in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, and reads them back on your other devices. Tijori cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Only encrypted files go into that folder (XChaCha20-Poly1305, with a key from Argon2id). They stay in your Google account. Google can see their number, sizes and change times, but not their contents. The details are on the security page.

The sign-in token Google gives Tijori is kept on your device: on Windows, in Tijori's own folder, encrypted with Windows' data protection, until you sign out; on the phone, in the browser's storage until it expires, about an hour later.

04Use of Google user data

Tijori's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain words: Tijori uses Google data only to sync your own vault between your own devices. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone, and never read by a person, because it never reaches us at all.

05Other connections

Apart from Google, the app connects to the internet only for these, and none of them carries your documents or passwords:

  • Update checks (the installed Windows app): the app asks GitHub for the latest release details. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request.
  • Breach check, only if you turn it on (it is off by default): the app sends the first 5 characters of each password's SHA-1 hash to Have I Been Pwned (api.pwnedpasswords.com) and compares the answer on your device. Your passwords and their full hashes never leave it.
  • Voice-to-text, if you use it: a one-time download of a speech model from Hugging Face and its engine from jsDelivr. After that it works offline.
  • Reading documents on the phone: the first time, the phone app downloads its text-reading engine and language data from jsDelivr. The reading itself happens on the phone.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Tijori sends them nothing about you beyond the request itself.

06This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep server logs, including IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

07Sharing and selling

We do not collect personal data, so we have none to share or sell. When you share a document from Tijori, for example on WhatsApp, it goes only where you send it.

08Removing your data

  • Stop syncing: sign out in Tijori's sync settings.
  • Delete the Drive copy: open Google Drive on the web, then Settings, Manage apps, Tijori, Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Tijori's access to your Google account: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Tijori.
  • On Windows: delete the vault folder, %APPDATA%\com.angad.tijori, or tick "Delete the application data" when you uninstall.
  • On the phone: clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

Deleted data cannot be recovered by us, because we never had a copy.

09Children

Tijori collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Families often keep children's documents in a vault; those stay encrypted on the family's own devices and Drive.

10Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Tijori ever asks Google for a new permission, this page will say so before the release that needs it.

11Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, open an issue at github.com/angadseth/tijori/issues.
