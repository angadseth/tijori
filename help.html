01Install on Windows

  1. Download the installer from the download page. If you like, check its SHA-256 first.
  2. Open it. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, see the SmartScreen prompt. Tijori installs for your account only, so it won't ask for an administrator password.
  3. Open Tijori from the Start menu and make your vault. Choose a master password of at least 8 characters; a few unrelated words make a strong one that is easy to remember.
  4. Tijori then shows your recovery key once. Save the Recovery Kit PDF and print it, or write the key down, before you go on.

Prefer not to install? The portable app on the download page runs as a single file and opens the same vault.

02Set up the phone app

The phone app installs from the browser, with no app store. Open angadseth.github.io/tijori/app on your phone, then:

  • Android, in Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • iPhone, in Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Open Tijori from your home screen. If your PC already syncs, choose Open my vault from Google and type your master password. Otherwise, start a new vault on the phone.

Good to know

On the phone, the vault lives in the browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data, or the browser's data, removes it from the phone. Your Drive copy stays, so you can open it again.

03Sync your PC and phone

  1. On your PC, open Tijori's sync settings and choose to sign in with Google.
  2. Google opens in your browser. Pick your account and allow Tijori to use its own hidden folder in your Drive. It cannot see your other files.
  3. Tijori uploads your sealed vault: first the list of your items, then the documents. A big vault can take a few minutes the first time.
  4. On the phone, choose Open my vault from Google, sign in with the same Google account and type the same master password.

After that, changes reach your other devices at the next sync. If the same item changed on two devices, the newer change wins. On the phone, Google's sign-in lasts about an hour; after that, tap the cloud button to sync again.

04Your recovery key

When you make a vault, Tijori shows a recovery key: 32 letters and numbers in eight groups of four. It is the only way back in if you forget your master password.

  • Write it on paper or print it, and keep it somewhere safe away from your PC, with your other important papers.
  • Don't keep it only inside Tijori, or in a photo on the same phone.
  • Capital and small letters don't matter when you type it, and Tijori treats O as 0 and I or L as 1.
  • Each time you use it, Tijori gives you a new one and the old one stops working. Replace your paper copy.
  • Lost the paper, or worried someone saw it? Open Settings, Security and choose New key. Tijori asks for your master password, makes a new key and Recovery Kit, and the old key stops working.

05The SmartScreen prompt

Windows may say Windows protected your PC the first time you open the installer. It does this for new programs without a paid code-signing certificate, and Tijori doesn't have one. Choose More info, check the app name, then Run anyway.

To be sure the file is genuine, compare its SHA-256 with the one on the download page. The download page also covers Edge and Chrome download warnings and Smart App Control.

06Forgot your password

  1. On the lock screen, choose the option to use your recovery key.
  2. Type the recovery key, then choose a new master password.
  3. Tijori opens your vault and shows a new recovery key. Write it down; the old one no longer works.

If you sync, your other devices will ask for the new password after they sync.

Without the recovery key

If you have lost both the master password and the recovery key, the vault cannot be opened, by you or by anyone else, including us. Nobody holds a copy of your key. This is what keeps the vault private.

07Moving to a new PC

If you sync

  1. Install Tijori on the new PC.
  2. Instead of making a new vault, choose to open your vault from Google, sign in with the same account and type your master password.
  3. Tijori downloads your sealed vault. Windows Hello has to be turned on again on the new PC.

If you don't sync

Make an encrypted backup from Tijori's backup settings onto a pen drive, install Tijori on the new PC and choose Restore a backup on its first screen. The backup stays sealed: it opens only with your master password or recovery key.

08Uninstalling safely

Uninstall from Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Tijori, Uninstall.

Your vault stays where it is, in %APPDATA%\com.angad.tijori, unless you tick Delete the application data in the uninstaller. Only tick it if you have a backup or a synced copy you can open, or if you really want the vault gone. Reinstalling Tijori later opens the vault that was left behind.

Your Drive copy is not touched by uninstalling. The privacy policy explains how to delete it.

09Still stuck

Open an issue at github.com/angadseth/tijori/issues and describe what you see. Never post your master password, recovery key or any document from your vault.
